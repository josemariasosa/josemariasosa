I was born in the desert of San Luis Potosí, México 🌵🇲🇽.

I am an engineer, builder, and curious operator interested in the space between software, hardware, and distributed systems.

These days, I spend most of my time exploring **Embedded Rust**, building hardware drivers, and working on **Metro Simulation Engine** — a small world where virtual trains, physical devices, radio messages, and deterministic simulations learn to coexist.

Some of the things I'm working on:

- **Metro Simulation Engine** — a deterministic simulation that connects virtual and physical railway systems.
- **Embedded Rust** — writing drivers, exploring registers, talking to sensors, cameras, and radio transceivers, one byte at a time.
- **Iron Burrow** — an underground infrastructure project for indexing blockchains, protecting nodes, and serving honest, source-aware data.

I enjoy understanding what happens beneath the abstractions: how a register controls a device, how a message travels through the air, and how small independent components become a working system.

I like systems that are simple, transparent, and resilient.  
I like software that tells the truth about what it knows.  
I like building things slowly enough to understand them.

**Somewhere between the bare metal and the burrow.**
